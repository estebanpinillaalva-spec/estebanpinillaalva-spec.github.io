AI-driven automation for companies

Processes someone repeats by hand every week, turned into something that works without depending on one person

I am an engineer. I look for the data and process problems every company has and solve them with automation, using AI where it adds value and deterministic code where reliability is needed.

How I work

  1. Measure the starting point. How long the process takes today, how often it is repeated and who does it. Without that figure there is no way to know whether the solution worked.
  2. Narrow the scope. One specific process, with an acceptance criterion written down before starting.
  3. Build with verification. The AI interprets the request; a deterministic engine executes and checks the result. Restricting the input usually fixes more than a cleverer formula.
  4. Hand over with documentation. A tutorial written for the people who will use it, the limitations in writing, and nothing that depends on me being there.
